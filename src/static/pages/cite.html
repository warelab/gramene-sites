Citing Gramene

The Gramene database is freely available to its users for conducting comparative plant genomic and pathway analysis. Users can upload their data on both Genome Browser and on the Plant Reactome (https://plantreactome.gramene.org) pathway browser for analysis and visualization. Additional tools available at Gramene include CMap Viewer, Expression Atlas, and Gramene Mart. 

When using Gramene or the information derived from Gramene databases, kindly acknowledge the Gramene project (http://www.gramene.org; date accessed and the database version). For information on linking directly to particular data items, see this document.
